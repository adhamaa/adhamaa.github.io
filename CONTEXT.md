# Portfolio

Adham Akmal Azmi's personal portfolio: who Adham is, what Adham has built, and how to hire Adham. It exists to win full-time or contract work.

## Language

### The site

**Site**:
The portfolio as a whole — every page at adhamaa.github.io.
_Avoid_: App, portfolio project; say "the Site's Project" only when meaning its entry in the work list

**Content**:
The words, lists and images about Adham that the owner edits without touching code: Profile, Projects, Roles and Stack Groups.
_Avoid_: Data, copy

**Draft**:
A Project or Case Study that is saved but not yet shown on the Site. Being a Draft says nothing about the Project's Status.
_Avoid_: Unpublished, hidden, private

### What Adham has done

**Project**:
One piece of work Adham built and shows in the work list, such as Sharah CMpro. The Site is itself one of the Projects.
_Avoid_: Work item, portfolio piece

**Slug**:
A Project's permanent name in its Case Study's address, fixed when the Project is created and kept even if the Project is renamed or reordered.
_Avoid_: ID (the two-digit number on the page is only its position)

**Status**:
Where the product behind a Project is in its life: shipped, active or archived. It describes the product, not whether the Project appears on the Site.
_Avoid_: State, published

**Case Study**:
An optional long-form write-up of a single Project, read on its own page. A Project has at most one.
_Avoid_: Post, article, deep dive

**Block**:
A ready-made element placed inside a Case Study: a Figure (one image with a caption), a Callout (a highlighted note) or a Gallery (several images).
_Avoid_: Component, widget, shortcode, embed

**Role**:
One job Adham held at a company, with its period, summary and points.
_Avoid_: Job, position, experience entry

**Stack Group**:
A named cluster of tools Adham works with, such as Frontend.
_Avoid_: Skill category, tech section

### Who Adham is

**Profile**:
The facts about Adham as a person — identity, contact details, availability, tagline, bio and principles.
_Avoid_: About, bio (the bio is one part of the Profile)

**Résumé**:
The downloadable PDF of Adham's career, produced separately from the Site's Content. It can drift out of sync with Roles.
_Avoid_: CV
